\section{Formulations Under the Naive-Attacker Standard}
\label{sec:phase4}

\draftnote{OUTLINE ONLY, per the PI's explicit instruction: leave this

This section will compare seven fairness-loss formulations (the
original pairwise-threshold baseline, a soft-weighted Lipschitz
individual-fairness penalty, a floor loss, a variance-of-risk
regularizer, group-DRO, per-user minimax, and a new group-aware
adversarial-training architecture) against plain Mo-PAE and a
no-privacy utility-ceiling reference, under the naive-attacker standard
of Section~\ref{sec:threat-model-naive}, once the approved Checkpoint
04d batch finishes.

  the two end-points of the achievable utility range.}
\item \draftnote{A leveling-down check (fraction of users helped,
  harmed, or unchanged relative to plain Mo-PAE at matched utility,

  Checkpoint 04c's draft claims.}
\item \draftnote{Report $\geq 3$ encoder seeds' worth of variance for

\end{itemize}
